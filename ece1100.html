Project Progress

Throughout the semester, the project evolved through distinct phases:

  • Research and Planning: Defining system requirements, component selection, and initial schematic drafting.
  • Prototyping: Building preliminary breadboard circuits and writing foundational code/logic.
  • Integration and Testing: Bringing hardware and software together, troubleshooting errors, and refining performance.

Project Successes and Failures

Every engineering project comes with its unique set of challenges and breakthroughs:

  • Successes: Successfully integrating the core subsystems and achieving stable baseline functionality.
  • Failures & Roadblocks: Encountered initial issues with component power regulation and debugging logic errors, which required iterative redesigns and careful testing.

ECE Skills Gained

Working on this project allowed me to apply and expand several key technical competencies, including:

  • Circuit design, analysis, and hardware troubleshooting.
  • Microcontroller programming and debugging.
  • Effective project management, documentation, and iterative problem-solving.

Final Thoughts

Completing this project provided invaluable hands-on insight into the engineering design process, reinforcing my interest in my chosen thread and confirming my path in electrical and computer engineering. Moving forward, I am excited about the possibility of continuing development on this project or scaling it into future endeavors.
